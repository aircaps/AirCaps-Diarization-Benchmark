## Implementations and weights

- [DiariZen](https://github.com/BUTSpeechFIT/DiariZen), BUT Speech@FIT; [Large v2 weights](https://huggingface.co/BUT-FIT/diarizen-wavlm-large-s80-md-v2).
- [NeMo](https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo), NVIDIA; [Nemotron 3 Diarization model card](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Nemotron-3-Diarization), including training-data disclosure.
- [pyannote.audio](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-audio); [3.1](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-3.1) and [Community-1](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1).
- [3D-Speaker](https://github.com/modelscope/3D-Speaker), Alibaba; pinned upstream dependencies and model identifiers are documented in `docs/INFERENCE.md`.
- [pyannote.metrics](https://github.com/pyannote/pyannote-metrics) provides DER scoring. See its citation guidance for the metric implementation.

## Published result sources

Precision-3 and unmeasured pyannote baseline cells come from the [Community-1 benchmark table](https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1#benchmark). Unmeasured 3D-Speaker cells are the reference results recorded by the [DiariZen Large v2 release](https://huggingface.co/BUT-FIT/diarizen-wavlm-large-s80-md-v2). They are labeled published-only and cannot be reconstructed from predictions in this repository.
